Free Online Image Compressor

TBolt Image Compressor is a free online tool that helps you reduce image file sizes without installing additional software. Compress JPG, JPEG, PNG and WebP images directly in your browser while preserving the original image format.

Compress JPG and JPEG Images

Reduce the file size of JPG and JPEG photos by adjusting compression quality. Download your compressed image in its original format without manually converting it.

Compress PNG Images

Compress PNG images while maintaining their PNG format. PNG uses lossless encoding in this tool, so the quality slider does not apply to PNG files.

Compress WebP Images

Reduce the size of WebP images with adjustable quality. The compressed result remains in WebP format.

No Signup or Installation Required

Start compressing images without creating an account. TBolt works directly in your browser, making it convenient for students, creators, website owners and everyday users.

How to Compress an Image?

First, upload your image using the browse button or drag-and-drop area. Adjust the compression quality, click Compress Image and download your compressed result. The original image format is automatically preserved.
